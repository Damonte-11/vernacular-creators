## Direction contract

THESIS: A creator tool where the data is the loudest thing on the page. It refuses the previous arrangement: every fact in its own rounded white card on a cream page.

OWN-WORLD: White pages, an Alabaster side menu, Eerie Black actions, Orange only on what is active or measured. Flat hairline panels, 8px and 6px corners, 32px controls, 13 to 14px Outfit, underlined tabs, status colour always with a word.

STORY: A campaign manager opens the tool, finds creators with the side filters and sortable columns, opens a profile, reads the score and the reasons under tabs, and adds the creator to a list, without scrolling past decoration.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Side menu (search, grouped links, settings and account at the bottom). Page header with title, one line and actions. Then the page's working surface: on Creators, a 232px filter column and a dense sortable table with removable filter chips above it.

FORM: The category standard, played straight, chosen by the user over the dealt directions (standing exit). Sits alongside Modash, HypeAuditor and Linear. seed: none (pinned) "Modash / Linear style".

MEMORABLE MOMENT: the views-guarantee pace bar on a Chorus project (orange fill, black notch for today's pace).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
