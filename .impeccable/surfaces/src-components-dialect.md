Mode: operate

Scope: Dialect projects (Vernacular manages a brand's own social pages month after month). Brand page, logging posts, the needs-attention list, creating a project with agreement targets, and the monthly report shown as 16:9 slides that go out as an editable Google Slides deck. Team only; clients never log in.

Task: a manager opens a brand most days to see this month against its target, logs new post links (content type and purpose are required), clears what needs attention (possible ads over 400K views, carousel numbers the apps hide, missing types, commitments), and on the 1st checks the generated report before downloading it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Dialect brand page answers "is this month on pace for its target?" with a living chart, not a number: organic views climbing day by day against a dashed on-pace line to the month's target. It refuses the grid of equal KPI cards.

OWN-WORLD: The existing Vernacular Creators world unchanged: white ground, Alabaster rail, hairline panels, Eerie Black actions, Vernacular Orange only for the measured line and active marks, Outfit with tabular figures, status colours only with words.

STORY: The manager reads the curve against the dashed line (on pace, ahead, behind by X), glances at the other three targets beside it, logs today's links, clears the needs-attention list and moves on.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (brand, Dialect, platforms, agreement month N of M, Needs attention count, Log a post); tabs (this month, Posts, Report, Brief and documents, Settings); the pace chart with posts-per-day bars and the end-of-month target; followers, posts and engagements targets with pace notches beside it; latest posts table and the needs-attention list below.

SIGNATURE MOVE: the month pace chart: an orange cumulative line, a dashed black on-pace line to the target, a dot with today's total and a verdict in words; possible ads are drawn outside the line until a manager decides.

FORM: Three layouts behind a Layout switch on the month tab, remembered per person: Timeline (A, the default: the agreement's months as a strip, then the four targets), Platforms (B: a column per platform) and Pace chart (C, the dealt structure the user first picked: the signature chart leads).
